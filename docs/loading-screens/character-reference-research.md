**Checked:** 2026-10-14 (Asia/Saigon)  
**Scope:** Official visual/identity references for the requested anime-style GTA loading-screen roster and its wordmark direction. Research only. No artwork was downloaded, generated, or edited, and no game files were touched.

## Result

The requested roster contains **23 distinct names**. Official Genshin/HoYoverse material was located for all 23, including Tsaritsa, Columbina, and Vesna. For most characters, use the official character directory for their nation as the visual index; it provides official character portraits and short bios. For Tsaritsa, use the official *Sudden Snow* trailer as the visual reference; the character directory does not provide her as a normal character portrait. Do not substitute fan art for any of these official sources.

The official site calls the first name **Hu Tao** (two words); the user spelling “Hutao” maps to the same official character. Keep the user’s roster as given in the planning brief, while using canonical source spellings in captions and reference links.

## Official logo reference for the title treatment

The official [Genshin Impact site home](https://genshin.hoyoverse.com/en/home) and [game overview](https://genshin.hoyoverse.com/m/en/game) display the official Genshin Impact wordmark/logo. Use those pages as the visual source when exploring “Grand Theft Auto Wibu World” / “Project Wibu” title lettering. A fantasy serif treatment is a design direction for the derivative title; no official source identifies the Genshin wordmark’s font, so this research does not assign it a font name. Do not reproduce unrelated logos as though they were Genshin’s official mark.

## Source interpretation and limits

- The character directory pages group characters by nation and are suitable as official identity and costume reference indexes. Their exact image assets were not downloaded.
- For Columbina and Vesna, the official teaser/trailer material gives more direct visual reference than a short directory bio. For Tsaritsa, the official *Sudden Snow* trailer is the visual source; the official anniversary voice-actor list provides a separate name/voice cross-check.
- A listed official visual source confirms that the character appears in official material. It does not establish that every detail in a future GTA illustration should be copied or that the source outfit is the user’s final preference. The loading-screen composition and GTA scene remain separate creative decisions.
- All sources above were opened or searched on 2026-10-14. These are live pages and may change after that date. No fan-made HoYoLAB posts, fan wikis, third-party asset mirrors, or unofficial art were used as evidence.
