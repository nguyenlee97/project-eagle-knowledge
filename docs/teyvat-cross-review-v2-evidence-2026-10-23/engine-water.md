# Q1 / Q5 refresh — engine bounds and water evidence

**Scope/status:** Read-only review of gameplay-lab configuration, its existing FLA/ModLoader logs, `data/water.dat`, and public reverse-engineered source. No game launch or game/staging mutation was performed. These artifacts confirm configured limits and a prior loader session; they do not prove a newly placed Teyvat build has been runtime-tested.

## Highest-priority findings

1. **The refreshed west edge is inside the configured world and water extents, with little water-grid edge margin.** `fastman92limitAdjuster_GTASA.ini` sets Paths, Radar, Water, RenderWare world, and World map sizes to 48,000; world sectors are 50 m and LOD sectors 200 m. The existing `fastman92limitAdjuster.log` records FLA 7.7 beta loading against `GTA SA 1.0 US HOODLUM` (14,383,616-byte executable), and confirms a 48,000 × 48,000 map, 960 × 960 50 m world sectors, 240 × 240 200 m LOD sectors, and 96 × 96 water blocks. On the centered ±24,000 m extent used by the config/data, `X=-23,667` is 333 m inside the west edge and lies in the outermost 500 m water block. It is not out of bounds, but it has only one partial water block between it and the boundary. At |X|≈23.7 km, IEEE-754 single-precision spacing is about 0.00195 m (1.95 mm), so float-coordinate quantization is not the primary placement risk at meter-scale terrain resolution.

2. **The 16 × 16 repeat-sector array is a periodic bucket hash, not an 800 m world-coverage boundary.** The prior claim that repeat-sector size caps the world’s simulation coverage should be removed. The existing FLA log records a 16 × 16 repeat-sector table; the GTA SA reverse-engineered `World.h` defines the vanilla table as 16 × 16 and `GetRepeatSector` indexes it with `abs(coord) % max`. With 50 m sectors, the table has an 800 m period per axis, so distant spatial sectors reuse buckets. That is a hash/candidate-list mechanism, not a stop line at ±400 m or ±800 m; the world sector scan uses the separate, extended 960 × 960 table in the current FLA log. Bucket density/collision-check cost and actual path/population data remain separate concerns. Specifically, the INI says changing Paths map size requires a new set of files; `gta.dat` references the existing `S&S\CDimages\default\paths.img`. The 48 km path-map patch and the log’s 64 × 64 path-tile count therefore do not establish that Teyvat has road/pedestrian nodes or continent-wide traffic routes. The cited source is a community reimplementation of SA 1.0 US, not a complete reverse-engineering claim about every FLA hook in this executable.

3. **The active native water data includes a zero-height sea surface at the western edge and around the proposed Mondstadt anchor, but the full rectangular Teyvat envelope is not uniformly covered by that surface.** `modloader.log` says it loaded `DATA\water.dat`. In that file, rows 40–41 define large all-zero-height quads: row 40 covers the full X range from -24,000 to +24,000 for Y=-24,000…-11,160; row 41 covers X=-24,000…-4,800 for Y=-11,160…+14,812. Their union covers the projected west-edge line `X=-23,667` across the proposed Y envelope and includes Mondstadt’s anchor neighborhood `(-6,500,-3,500)`. Row 41 stops at X=-4,800, though, so this is not evidence that every point in the full X=-23,667…-2,163 and Y=-12,943…+4,465 rectangular envelope is native sea. Verify water/land coexistence against actual terrain triangles rather than extending the outer-ocean assumption across the whole bbox.

4. **The file’s polygon counts fit the configured polygon/vertex capacities by a broad margin; water-list capacity cannot be inferred from those counts.** Parsing the loaded `water.dat` gives 2,019 four-vertex quad records and 6 three-vertex triangle records (8,094 vertex references). Applying the reverse-engineered loader’s int-cast X/Y and float Z key plus its coordinate/Z deduplication yields 6,425 distinct vertex keys. The current FLA settings are 30,100 quads, 999 triangles, 15,200 vertices, and 9,999 entries for “Water quads and triangles list”; the FLA log confirms these limits were patched. The data therefore uses 6.7% of the quad limit, 0.6% of the triangle limit, and about 42.3% of the vertex limit on that source-based estimate. **Do not report 8,094 as actual allocated vertices or infer list usage from 2,025 records.** The reverse-engineered source deduplicates vertices, then builds block-to-polygon combinations through `FillQuadsAndTrianglesList`; the current FLA log does not report combo-list occupancy or accepted/rejected record counts. Those remain runtime-unproven.

5. **Use native water geometry for swimming/query behavior; use an alpha mesh only for appearance.** The game’s water subsystem loads water records into quad/triangle/vertex structures and exposes level/depth queries (`GetWaterLevel`, `GetWaterLevelNoWaves`, `GetWaterDepth`); a DFF/alpha material does not register a swim plane with those systems. For a raised lake, define its native water level in `water.dat` and build an intentional footprint. At XY shared with the existing Z=0 ocean polygons, split/trim/replace the ocean polygons so the same point does not receive overlapping sea-level and +1.5 m surfaces. A transparent DXT5 surface may be layered for artistic styling after the gameplay surface is decided, but do not use it as a substitute for native water. The cited source exposes the query API and record model; this review did not test Aether’s swim animation or a +1.5 m pool in-game. No exact Cider Lake GTA height can be derived from the available files; +1.5 m remains a design proposal, not a measured source value.

## Practical decision for this plan

The anchor is within the logged map limits, and the west-edge placement has native Z=0 water beneath it. Keep the repeat-sector warning framed as possible bucket-density/performance and path/population-data risks, not a hard map-coverage ceiling. For Cider Lake, decide whether the local surface stays at the existing Z=0 ocean level or is a raised native-water lake; if raised, make the Z=0 polygons mutually exclusive at that footprint before adding the elevated water. Validate actual movement/swimming and water queries in the intended patched executable before calling it proven.
