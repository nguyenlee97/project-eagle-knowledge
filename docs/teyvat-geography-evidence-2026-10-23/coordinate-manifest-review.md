## Confirmed findings

- **Upstream region naming supports only a broad Natlan color bucket for HJCHS/BGC.** HoyoMap’s public `REGION_PATTERNS` assigns names containing `HJC` and `BGC` to the same generic color rule labelled Natlan; it does not distinguish HJCHS from Nod-Krai/BGCWBM, nor establish the expansion “Sacred Flame Stadium.” The frontend’s `getRegion()` is a regex-based material-color selection, not an authoritative region-identity registry ([upstream source, lines 89–99 and 264–268](https://github.com/flatypus/hoyomap/blob/main/public/index.html#L89-L99)). Thus the Natlan bucket is supported, but the precise place-name is not independently confirmed by this evidence.
- **XKC02 is not classified as Sumeru by the upstream filename rules.** The Sumeru rule lists `XM`, `Sumeru`, `SXT`, `CWL`, and other patterns, but not `XKC`; unmatched names receive a default gray color ([same source, lines 89–99 and 264–268](https://github.com/flatypus/hoyomap/blob/main/public/index.html#L89-L99)). This does not prove XKC02 is not Sumeru; it means the plan’s label needs a separate identity source.
- **XKC02 is not one complete single-LOD city asset.** Five separate all-HLOD entries share manifest position `(-367, 364, 2843)`: Building Lod0, City Lod1+Lod2, Leaf Lod0, Leaf ShadowMesh key `0`, and Stone Lod0. Manifest JSON has the exact names and LOD filenames. A table row treating this as one `Area_XKC02` anchor hides this split and the fact the central `City` group has no Lod0.
- **HJCHS and BGCWBM have distinct manifest anchors, but the HoyoMap region color rule merges their prefixes.** HJCHS is `(-168.45, 387.83, 10041.58)`; BGCWBM is `(3263, 328, 9903)`. BGCWBM also has separate `NK_ARSE_P1` and `NK_ARSE_P2` festival entries at the same anchor. This makes the source useful for a Natlan-bucket annotation but insufficient to decide whether a mesh is Natlan proper or Nod-Krai.
- **The city placement transform is a separate mechanism from HLOD-grid placement.** Public `loadCityMesh()` places every city OBJ at `(-entry.position.x + 1024, entry.position.y, entry.position.z)`; the city entry initializer repeats that world position calculation ([upstream source, lines 465–500 and 605–617](https://github.com/flatypus/hoyomap/blob/main/public/index.html#L465-L500)). In contrast, block HLOD uses grid coordinates and centers each mesh from its bounding-box center (`blockCenter` and `loadBlockMesh`, lines 351–353 and 430–438). Therefore a formula inferred from city manifest anchors must not silently be applied to grid HLOD or to raw OBJ vertex positions.
- **Available Mondstadt OBJ LODs do not share an obvious vertex-origin convention.** In this snapshot, Lod0 vertices for `Area_Mengde_City_Constant_HLOD` span approximately X `[-137.7, 135.8]`, Z `[-187.1, 187.3]`; Lod1 and Lod2 instead span X `[-2412.7, -2139.2]`, Z `[-970.1, -595.7]`. The manifest anchor is `(2275,263,-783)`. The public loader applies the same manifest offset regardless of LOD. This demonstrates materially different raw coordinate ranges across LOD files; without validating the resulting placed bounds against the source map, one transform for all raw OBJs is not established. Lod0 and Lod1 bounds are included in the JSON output.
- **Coverage metrics need careful wording.** The 196 Android HLOD keys occupy an inclusive key envelope of 17 × 21 = 357 possible cells, with 161 absent keys inside that rectangle. They are not a continuous 196-cell rectangular coverage. In the 291 city entries, 97 have exact zero position and 34 lack Lod0 (6 are in both groups). LOD signatures: 106 Lod0-only, 117 Lod0+1, 34 Lod0+1+2, 23 Lod1-only, 3 Lod1+2, and 8 Lod2-only. These counts describe manifest records, not downloadable-file validation or visual map coverage.
- **The local override file materially changes some placements.** It contains 13 ID-substring overrides, including city mesh-specific positions and grid HLOD overrides. For example, `Stages_BigWorld_0_4_Constant_HLOD` is overridden to `(1016.38, 0, 4103.79)` and `Stages_BigWorld_-4_5_Constant_HLOD` to `(5171.28, 0, 5110.28)`. Combined with the loader’s per-block bounding-box centering, this means grid position is not just a direct application of the manifest `bx_bz` key; any claimed surveyed coverage must account for these overrides and actual mesh bounds.

## Coordinate implications for the master-plan review

The manifest positions themselves show why geography/image alignment should be tested before accepting the master-plan row assignments. Relative to the `Area_Mengde` anchor, the coordinate differences `(delta x, delta z)` are: `Area_LYG=(-2885,+1073)`, `Area_XKC02=(-2642,+3626)`, `Area_HJCHS=(-2443,+10824)`, and `Area_BGCWBM=(+988,+10686)`. These are direct arithmetic differences between manifest anchor fields, not claims about GTA axes or raw OBJ vertices. Under the parent review’s proposed image basis (east `=-delta z`, north `=+delta x`), those become XKC02 southwest `(-3626,-2642)`, HJCHS southwest `(-10824,-2443)`, and BGCWBM northwest `(-10686,+988)`. That ordering is qualitatively different from reading positive `z` as north and positive `x` as east. It is a useful coordinate sanity check, not proof that every mesh’s vertices share the manifest anchor frame.

The master-plan table’s displayed derived coordinates also need a source-column audit: its stated equations at lines 23–25 are functions of `X_obj`, `Z_obj`, and `Y_obj`, while the “Anchor (HoyoMap)” column at lines 37/39/40 gives manifest position triples. Those are not interchangeable by definition; the local Lod0/Lod1 bounds and upstream loader behavior make that distinction material. I have not attempted to fit the parent’s screenshot or infer mesh extents from origins.

## Limits / unresolved

- HoyoMap’s filename-color mapping is an implementation hint, not a canonical HoYoverse place-name table. `HJC` in the Natlan pattern supports broad categorization but cannot prove a proper-noun expansion or distinguish content introduced in different updates. The `NK_ARSE` festival suffix is strong evidence the BGCWBM family includes Nod-Krai-labeled assets, while still not proving every `BGC` asset is Nod-Krai.
- Local CDN snapshot and current public GitHub `main` may differ by revision. The upstream source is cited for code behavior; manifest counts are from the specified local snapshot.
- Missing LOD0 or zero position does not mean an object is absent from the live map. It may have a fallback LOD, be a separate mesh group, require an override, or need a different origin convention.
- Mesh-origin checks were limited to locally present constant-city OBJ LOD0/1/2 files. No HJCHS, XKC02, or BGCWBM mesh download was made. The HoyoMap browser rendering and atlas image fit remain for the parent to verify.
