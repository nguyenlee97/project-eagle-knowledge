# Genshin worldbuilding foundation for character knowledge

**Checked:** 2026-10-24  
**Snapshot:** Released story through Version 7.1.  
**Purpose:** Give the character-chat system a small, connected model of Teyvat’s cosmology, history, and memory rules. This is a research foundation, not an exhaustive lore database. Story spoilers through 7.1.

## Reading the evidence

Most detailed lore is presented through in-game books, character stories, and quest dialogue. The pages below are community-hosted transcriptions of those primary texts, or official HoYoWiki summaries; the hosting site is not the game. The Enkanomiya chronicle *Before Sun and Moon* is itself an in-world account with gaps and claims about ancient history, not a narrator outside the story who guarantees every inference. Moriasmoday is useful for locating topics and quest references, but its informal paraphrase and speculation should not be treated as primary evidence.

The release version is a **knowledge cutoff**, not the in-world date of every fact. A character may know a 7.1 outcome that happened recently in the story and know a 2.4 book’s account of much older history. Those are separate timeline axes.

## Knowledge tiers for the product

1. **Shared story canon, snapshot 7.1:** Retrieval can provide settled world facts to every selected character. This is the deliberate product setting. It does not assert that every ordinary person in Teyvat knows every fact.
2. **Character-specific first-hand memory:** Use “I saw,” “I remember,” or “I met” only for experiences established for that character. Venti’s 5.8 dialogue supports his own meeting with “Time” at the Thousand Winds Temple and receiving fragments of power/authority. It does not establish that he attended the Snezhnaya confrontation or has unrestricted access to Istaroth’s knowledge.
3. **Character interpretation and disposition:** Each persona can react differently to a shared fact. The fact layer should not force Jean, Diluc, Mona, or Venti to sound alike or hold identical opinions.
4. **Unsettled or speaker-attributed material:** Preserve who said it and the uncertainty. Avoid upgrading a book’s author, an antagonist’s explanation, a character’s hypothesis, or a community summary into absolute narration.
5. **User-specific relationship memory:** Store what this player told the character, what the character promised, and acknowledged shared experiences. This is private interaction history; it cannot alter canon facts, another character’s memory, or the in-game timeline.

For the specific requested Venti behavior, his prompt should say that the 7.1 world reference is available to him, including the Four Shades, the other Archons, the Heavenly Principles, and the Ronova event. It should also say to avoid claiming eyewitness participation unless a source establishes it. This resolves the product requirement without grounding his knowledge in unsupported omniscience or a fabricated news-delivery mechanism.

## Uncertainty register

- **The source and status of ancient cosmology:** *Before Sun and Moon* is an in-world historical account. Treat its assertions as important evidence, not a perfectly complete external narrator. Later character memories add information rather than erase the need to mark the source.
- **Primordial One and Heavenly Principles:** *Before Sun and Moon* calls the ancient creator the Primordial One; Focalors’s checked 4.2 dialogue discusses the Heavenly Principles but does not state the equation directly. A community wiki change note attributes confirmation to Focalors, but the exact primary line was not found in the transcript. Keep the terms separate or mark the equation as secondary-source reported until directly verified. The book presents “Phanes” as a possible name, not a settled universal alias.
- **Authority versus possession:** Dragon elemental Authorities, the four older Life/Death/Time/Space authorities, Archon divine seats, and Gnoses interact. Their precise lineage and mechanics are not fully explained. The 7.1 moon account should be represented as a revealed layer, not a completed causal diagram.
- **Descender criteria and identity:** Nahida’s report of Fatui classification and Rene’s world-rivaling-will criterion are distinct sources with distinct speakers. The Traveler’s sibling’s external origin and Irminsul entry should both remain visible; do not invent a resolution or promote either speaker’s account into a fully reconciled universal definition.
- **7.1 public knowledge:** The game establishes the event for the player and this product’s requested world snapshot. It does not establish an in-world delivery route from Snezhnaya to Mondstadt. The character system may make the story retrievable without asserting that every ordinary Teyvat resident has heard it.
- **Ronova’s fate:** Official promotion and accessible community coverage do not settle her death. Retain “defeated/disappeared; final status unconfirmed” unless a later released primary text resolves it.

## Practical retrieval policy

Keep this as a small shared lore reference, not a giant fixed prompt. Each retrieved record should carry its release floor, source/type, confidence, and whether it is first-hand, reported, or interpretation. The persona prompt controls voice and character-specific stance; it should not override a better-sourced fact. Store chat memories in a separate private layer, so a player’s personal history can persist without turning their recollection into Teyvat’s canon or Irminsul’s record.
