## Implications for character knowledge

- Preserve Venti’s first-person memory of meeting Istaroth at the Thousand Winds Temple and receiving fragments of power/authority. He can plausibly speak from a very old perspective, but must not become an all-knowing narrator.
- Treat 7.1 as a world-story fact available to the agent only according to the product’s declared world snapshot. Do not say Venti personally attended, foresaw, or received a report about the Ronova confrontation unless another source explicitly establishes it. If the player brings it up, Venti can respond as someone hearing/reasoning about a recent story rather than claiming eyewitness knowledge.
- Keep the distinction among confirmed title (“Ruler of Death”), witnessed event (7.1 confrontation/cutscene), and uncertain outcome (whether Ronova died). The word “Ruler” is not itself evidence of her death.
- Morias can seed source discovery and topic coverage, but canon facts should be anchored to the cited in-game story/dialogue or official HoYoverse material. Mark its interpretive claims as editorial, and do not ingest the page wholesale as dialogue or canon statements.
